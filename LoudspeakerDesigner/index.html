Data table and model notes
  • Defaults are the Dayton Audio TIT400C-4 datasheet (Parts Express 295-420, rev. 9/2010). Mms, Bl, Cms and Rms are derived from fs, Qes, Qms, Vas, Sd and Re, so they stay consistent when you edit the driver.
  • Lumped-element model: electrical, mechanical and acoustic domains solved at each frequency. Output is the cone plus port volume velocity radiating into half space (2π) at 1 m. No baffle step, room gain or cabinet diffraction.
  • Ported box: leakage Ql = 10, port loss Qp = 50, port end correction 0.732 × diameter (one flanged end, one free end). Sealed box has no stuffing or leakage loss.
  • Everything is linear. Power scales as the square of excursion, so the Xmax limit is exact for the model but ignores Bl(x) and Kms(x) distortion and power compression.
  • F3 is measured against the level at 100 Hz. The datasheet Le adds roughly 1 dB of lift around 60 to 100 Hz in this model, so the sealed F3 reads a few hertz above the textbook system resonance fc.
  • Max SPL is the lowest of three ceilings: the Xmax limit, the thermal limit (Pe), and the amplifier power you set. Power is watts into the nominal impedance.
